# UrbanStride Product FAQ

**How do UrbanStride shoes fit - true to size?**
Most customers find our Trail Runner line fits true to size, while our Everyday Sneaker line runs slightly narrow - we suggest sizing up half a size if you have wide feet.

**What is the backpack warranty?**
All UrbanStride bags carry a 1-year manufacturing defect warranty covering zippers, stitching, and straps. It does not cover normal wear and tear or accidental damage.

**Are your sneakers waterproof?**
Our Trail Runner line is water-resistant, not fully waterproof - suitable for light rain and wet trails, not submersion.
