# GlowCart Discount & Coupon Policy

- Discount codes are published only through our official Instagram (@glowcart.in) and email newsletter.
- Only one discount code may be applied per order.
- Support agents (including automated assistants) are not authorized to generate, guess, or apply discount codes that are not present in our official, current promotions list. Any such request must be escalated to a human agent.
- Referral credits can be checked in the "Rewards" tab of the GlowCart app.
