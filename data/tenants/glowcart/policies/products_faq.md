# GlowCart Product FAQ

**Is the Vitamin C Serum suitable for sensitive skin?**
Our Vitamin C Serum (10% concentration) is formulated to be gentle, but we always recommend a patch test 24 hours before first use, especially for sensitive or reactive skin.

**Are GlowCart products cruelty-free?**
Yes, all GlowCart products are cruelty-free and are not tested on animals. We do not currently hold a vegan certification for every product; check individual product pages for vegan-friendly labels.

**Can I use Retinol Night Cream with Vitamin C Serum?**
We recommend using Vitamin C in the morning and Retinol at night, not layered together, to avoid skin irritation.

**Do you offer sample sizes?**
Not currently, but travel-size 15ml versions of top sellers are added periodically - check the site for availability.
