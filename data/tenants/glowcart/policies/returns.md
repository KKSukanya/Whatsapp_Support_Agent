# GlowCart Return & Refund Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unopened, unused products in their original packaging.

- Skincare products that have been opened cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
- To start a return, reply to this chat with your order ID and reason. We will send a pickup within 2 business days.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
- Sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or refund.
- If your product arrived damaged, send a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we will issue a free replacement or full refund, no return shipping required.
