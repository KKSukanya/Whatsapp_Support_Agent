# GlowCart Shipping Policy

- Standard shipping across India takes 4-6 business days.
- Orders above ₹999 ship free. Orders below ₹999 have a flat ₹49 shipping fee.
- We currently do not ship internationally.
- Once an order is packed, the address cannot be changed. Please contact support immediately after placing the order if you need to make a correction.
- Tracking links are sent via WhatsApp and email once the order is shipped, usually within 24-48 hours of placing the order.
